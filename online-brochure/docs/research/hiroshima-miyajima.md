- The quote is now the real Cenotaph epitaph (Tadayoshi Saika, with the city's translation).

- "Genbaku Dome" is now called the Atomic Bomb Dome (with 原爆ドーム), as Hiroshima City does in English.
- The old agenda's "torii lit after sunset" line dropped (illumination times not re-checked).
