Second editor, offline (see the status note at the top). ✔ = agrees with the checker's own

### Corrections made

| Where | Old | New | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shibuya Sky (p3) | "229 m above the station" | "229 m above the street" | 229 m (229.71 m) is the building's height above ground (row 1 sources, Wikipedia: Shibuya Scramble Square). The station's platforms sit at several different levels, so "above the station" was loose. |
| Costs (p10), Shibuya Sky | "Under-5s free" | "Book sunset slots early" | Not in the row 3 sources as printed (the log itself says children's tickets were not printed). The checker recalls a separate paid band for small children with only under-3s free, so the age cut-off is disputed: removed. The new bullet is advice, not a fact. |
| Features (p6), Omoide Yokochō | "about 60 smoky counters, most of them grilling…" | "dozens of smoky counters, many grilling…" | Counts disagree: about 60 (official history page, per row 13), about 80 (the figure the checker recalls from Japanese-language sources) and about 100 (Fun-Japan, wider network). A non-numeric phrase is true under all three. |
| Features (p6), Tokyo Skytree | "tickets are timed" | "buy a timed ticket online" | Skytree sells date-and-time tickets online, but walk-up same-day tickets also exist, so "tickets are timed" overstated it. |
| Summary | "Golden Gai's 200 tiny bars" | "Golden Gai's 200-odd tiny bars" | Row 6: "around 200" (some guides 200–300). An exact count was implied. |
