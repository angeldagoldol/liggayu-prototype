## Changes from the old brochure

- Removed the fictional publisher "Matsuri Road Travel", its fake email and office, invented packages and prices
  (¥118,000 / ¥236,000), and the invented testimonial ("Tomás R.").
- Removed the stats page. "46 lanterns" and "6 days of Nebuta" were correct and live on in the copy.
- Old agenda: Akita on 4 Aug → Yamagata 6 Aug → Sendai 7 Aug. New route: Aomori 4 → Akita 5 → Sendai 6 →
  Yamadera/Yamagata 7 → Sendai 8, which avoids the slow Akita–Yamagata rail link.
- Added place pages for the big three, Hirosaki, Goshogawara, Morioka and Yamadera, a map, visitor information
  and a real haiku.
