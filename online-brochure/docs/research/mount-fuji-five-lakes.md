# Research log — Mount Fuji & the Five Lakes (`mount-fuji-five-lakes`)

Researched and written 17 October 2026 by Tabi Guides (new brochure). Research was done through web
search only: outside sites could not be opened directly from the research sandbox (WebFetch/curl
blocked), so every value below comes from search-result extracts of the pages listed. This brochure
shared one web-search budget with `japan-alps`: 38 searches in total for both.

`factsChecked: "October 2026"` is set. Every price, opening hour, date and access detail printed on the
pages is backed by at least one current source, and the surprising ones (the ¥4,000 hiking fee on all
four trails, the 2026 season dates) by official pages. Values where sources disagree are printed with
"about", as a range or more loosely, and are marked HEDGED below.

**Changed recently (do not copy older guides):** the fee is now **¥4,000** on all four trails, with
online pre-learning on the Shizuoka side (official pages, rows 23 and 26). The earlier values (Yoshida
toll ¥2,000 in 2024; a voluntary ¥1,000 donation on the Shizuoka trails) are the editor's background
knowledge, consistent with one 2026 guide that still prints ¥2,000 — they are context only and not
printed. In 2026 the Subashiri trail opens on **1 July** with Yoshida (blog sources say it used to open
on 10 July).

Status key: **VERIFIED** = printed value matches the sources listed · **HEDGED** = sources disagree or
only one weak source, so the copy uses "about", a range or a looser wording · **REMOVED** = could not
be verified, not printed.
